💡 Tips

  • Boost the low bands for a fuller, warmer sound; boost the high bands for extra clarity.
  • If vocals feel buried, try raising the 1kHz–4kHz range.
  • Start from a preset, then fine-tune with the sliders.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many bands can I adjust?

Five independent bands — 60Hz, 250Hz, 1kHz, 4kHz, and 12kHz — each adjustable from -20dB to +20dB.

How do the presets work?

Click a preset like Bass Boost, Vocal Boost, or Treble Boost to set the sliders automatically, then fine-tune them by hand if you like.

Is my file uploaded to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
